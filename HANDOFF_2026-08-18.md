## Loyalty behavior

- Calculation: 1 point per ฿20 actually spent on food/drinks, equivalent to 5 points per ฿100.
- Redemption: 1 point = ฿1 discount.
- Delivery fee does not earn points and cannot be paid with points.
- Points are computed at order creation only when a LINE user ID is attached.
- Points are credited after payment/slip verification via `lib/slipVerification.js` → `lib/pointsAward.js`.
- Payment-confirmed LINE Flex messages already show `+N แต้มสะสม`.
- Dine-in purchases are not automatically credited by this online flow.
- Read `lib/points.js`, `lib/pointsAward.js`, `pages/api/orders.js`, and `lib/orderFlex.js` before changing rules.
